Add a regular variety

Tap + Add variety on the home screen. Pick the right category (BP or DUST) at the top, then fill in name, grade, invoice, bags, Kg/Bag and rate. The app inserts the row in the correct master with all formulas set up for you.

Remember: OF means DUST. If a grade is OF, SOF, PF or PD, choose the DUST category. If you pick the wrong one, the app will warn you before saving.

Add an "Any Blend" variety

Any Blend teas (ANY BLEND DUP, RR, SHRI, JODHPUR, MIX, etc.) are pre-blended placeholders, not stock-tracked. To add one, open the master sheet and type a row in the Any Blend area with a name that starts with "ANY BLEND..." plus its invoice and rate. The app auto-detects these names and shows them in the blend picker.

Leaf input

For the leaf rows (Loose Sample / 950 KG fix), type the tea weight divided by 1000 — for example, if it's 850, type 0.85. The app multiplies it by the Kg/Bag (7 for BP, 6 for DUST) to get the leaf KG.

Draft vs Finalize

Draft is a trial — it does NOT touch your stock. Use it to plan a blend.

Finalize locks the bag counts and subtracts them from your godown stock. Once a blend is finalized it is frozen and cannot be edited. Later changes to the master only affect future blends, never a finalized one.

Delete a blend (same day only)

A blend can be deleted only on the day it was made. Open it in History — if the Delete button shows, you can remove it. Deleting it returns its bags to stock and removes its sheet. After the day passes, the blend is permanent.

Cross-category teas

In a BP blend you'll see BP teas plus an "Add tea from DUST" button (and the reverse in a DUST blend). A tea used across categories is always tracked against its own category's stock.

Stock date

When new stock of a variety arrives and you update its bags, its stock date updates automatically. Blends dated before that new date won't eat into the new stock — so a new lot starts fresh.

Zero stock

When a variety drops to zero or below, the app asks during Refresh stock whether to remove it. Confirm to delete it from the master.
